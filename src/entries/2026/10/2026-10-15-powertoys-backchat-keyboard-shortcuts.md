title: "Power Toys: BackChat moves in, keyboard shortcuts, 17.1 and 17.2"
date: 2026-10-15T11:30:00
---

**Repo:** [Jumoo/umbraco.community.powertoys](https://github.com/Jumoo/umbraco.community.powertoys)

- Brought BackChat into Power Toys as three power toys: BackChat chat, Tasks, and content reviews with reminders (#60, #61, #62); archived the old Jumoo/BackChat repo
- BackChat and Tasks panels now dock beside the page, like the Umbraco.AI Copilot sidebar
- Review check interval now applies without a restart
- Released [`17.1.0`](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Umbraco.Community.PowerToys/17.1.0) and wrote the [BackChats back](https://blog.jumoo.co.uk/2026/backchats-back/) blog post
- Added screenshots to the BackChat and Tasks docs
- New Keyboard Shortcuts power toy: `?` or a header button opens a docked panel listing the shortcuts on the current screen, read live from Umbraco (#68)
- Custom site-wide shortcuts: go to a section or URL, open search or a panel, run a workspace or entity action; Alt+1–9 go to sections by default; added and edited in their own sidebar (#70)
- Released [`17.2.0`](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Umbraco.Community.PowerToys/17.2.0) and added a keyboard shortcuts section to the blog post
