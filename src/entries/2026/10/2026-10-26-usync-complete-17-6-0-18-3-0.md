title: "uSync.Complete 17.6.0 and 18.3.0 released"
date: 2026-10-26T17:20:00
---

- uSync.Complete: 17.6.0 now requires uSync 17.5.0 and Jumoo.Processing 17.5.0; filled the changelog gaps since rc1
- Released [`uSync.Complete 17.6.0-rc2`](https://www.nuget.org/packages/uSync.Complete/17.6.0-rc2) to NuGet, with [public notes](https://github.com/Jumoo/uSync.Complete.Issues/releases/tag/v17.6.0-rc2)
- Released [`uSync.Complete 17.6.0`](https://www.nuget.org/packages/uSync.Complete/17.6.0) to NuGet, with [public notes](https://github.com/Jumoo/uSync.Complete.Issues/releases/tag/v17.6.0) (Publisher approvals, Sync Queue, `Publisher/Start` / `Exporter/Start` background API, skip unchanged media files, Umbraco 17.8 readiness)
- uSync.Complete: closed [#293](https://github.com/Jumoo/uSync.Complete.Issues/issues/293) and commented on [#310](https://github.com/Jumoo/uSync.Complete.Issues/issues/310) with the 17.6.0 fixes
- uSync.Complete: forward-ported v17/main into v18/main (73 commits): on Processing 18.3.0 / uSync 18.2.0, regenerated the Publisher and Exporter API clients against v18, approval status enum now serialises by name, approval migrations moved to `AsyncMigrationBase`
- uSync.Complete: fixed the v18 Background screen, whose process and queue lists never rendered, so scheduled runs can be seen and cancelled there
- Released [`uSync.Complete 18.3.0`](https://www.nuget.org/packages/uSync.Complete/18.3.0) to NuGet, with [public notes](https://github.com/Jumoo/uSync.Complete.Issues/releases/tag/v18.3.0)
